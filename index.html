About

SunRock Floors

Sunrock Floors is a locally owned flooring and tile company based in Castle Rock, Colorado, founded and operated by Grant Layman with the support of his wife and a team of skilled installation professionals.

After more than 15 years in the flooring and construction industry, Grant set out to build something lasting for his family and his community. Sunrock Floors was created from a simple idea: combine craftsmanship, integrity, and personal service to help people turn inspiration into beautiful, lasting space, one floor at a time.

Unlike traditional flooring companies, we keep things simple and focused. There is no big showroom or layers of overhead. Instead, we invest directly in what matters most: your project, your home, and the hands that bring it to life.

A portion of proceeds from every Sunrock project goes to the Tim Tebow Foundation.
